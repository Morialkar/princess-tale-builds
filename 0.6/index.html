Version 0.6

What changed since version 0.5.

2026-10-24iOS + Android

Clear messages when you are short.

Every purchase or upgrade you cannot afford now says what is missing, with the game's own message, instead of failing silently.

ImprovedFixUnder the hood

  • Skipping many stages in a row past the end of a chapter no longer sends the game back to the title screen in a loop. Accounts already stuck are repaired at the next login.

  • The home Skip waits at the end of a chapter until you go through the new chapter window.

  • Upgrading an artifact you cannot afford shows the game's “not enough items” or “not enough gold” message.

  • Every paid action you cannot afford (shops, summons, equipment, signature, events, guild, housing, dungeon) answers with the game's own message: not enough gold, stars, items, coins or crystals.
