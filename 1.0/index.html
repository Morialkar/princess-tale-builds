Princess Tale 1.0: the Cheats window.

User Info › Cheats: switches for the towers, the bosses and the home Skip, and buttons for packs, dailies, bounties, idle rewards and tickets, on iOS and Android.

NewImprovedFixUnder the hood

  • The Community button of User Info is now Cheats. Its window has switches for the towers' daily limit, the towers' days, unlimited boss tries, the home Skip minimum and a perfect Attic x10, and buttons to renew the packs, reset the daily limits or the daily missions, finish the bounties, get 24 hours of idle rewards and fill up the tickets.

  • Summon 100 is paid with stars when you have fewer than a hundred summon tickets, as Summon 10 is when the tickets run out.

  • The game keeps a copy of your save at startup, once a day and before every update that changes saved data: the last five are kept next to it.

  • Save repairs shipped with an update run once and completely, never twice or halfway.

  • A save that is busy at login no longer shows an empty roster, bag or balance: the game retries instead.

  • Screens answer faster: the simulated players are brought up to date once a day instead of at every request.

  • On Android, a server that cannot start says why on screen instead of leaving the game on its loading screen.

The Cheats window

Open User Info (your portrait on the home page) and press Cheats.

  1. Switches stay as you leave them: Skip, no minimum; Towers, no limit; Towers, every day; Unlimited bosses; Perfect Attic x10 (Armed or Disarmed).
  2. Buttons act once per press: Renew packs, Reset daily limits, Reset daily missions, Finish bounties, 24h idle rewards, Fill up tickets.
  3. What a button did shows next to it for a few seconds; idle rewards and tickets arrive in a Cheats mail.
  • Renew packs makes every shop pack buyable again, its first-purchase bonus included; the monthly pass and growth packages stay owned.
  • Reset daily missions lets you claim the day's missions again; the ones already done can be claimed at once.
  • The coupon codes still work.
