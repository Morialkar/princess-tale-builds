Princess Tale 1.2: Elyra, and two brides.

A new Light princess joins the Attic, Ladriel and Iliana get their wedding outfits, and your mailbox holds 100 Lucky Box Keys.

NewUnder the hood

  • A new princess, Elyra, Keeper of Lost Paths: a Light supporter who heals with her lantern. She can come out of the Attic, and the shop has her Package and her Special Package.

  • Two wedding outfits the original game planned but never finished: Bride Ladriel and Bride Iliana, each with its skin pack and a limited bundle.

  • At the first launch of 1.2, a mail brings a gift: 100 Lucky Box Keys, one Int, one Dex and one Str Stellar Monument, 50 Elements of Growth and 50 Elements of Evolution.

Elyra, Keeper of Lost Paths

A Light supporter from the border of Netherland, who keeps a light for everyone still on the road.

  • Wandering Spark, The Way Home, Lantern Dust, Stubborn Nightlight and A Light for Everyone: beacons, charm and healing.
  • Her signature weapon, the Lantern of the Missing, heals the whole team.
  • In the Attic's summons, and in the shop: Elyra Package and Elyra Special Package.

Your 1.2 gift

Open your mailbox after updating: the gift arrives once, at the first launch of 1.2.

  • 100 Lucky Box Keys, for the Attic.
  • Int, Dex and Str Stellar Monument: one of each, to advance an SS+ piece of equipment to SSS.
  • 50 Elements of Growth and 50 Elements of Evolution, for signature weapons.
