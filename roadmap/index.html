Version 0.5Planned

  • Shop goods rotate on each refresh and each day, not only the equipment: soul cards, timers and hero souls change.

  • The gold-for-dust offer grows with the shop tiers. From tier 4 the second shop row sells signature weapon materials (Growth in packs of 5, Evolution 2 to 4 at random, an element mark 1 to 3 at random), and the Colosseum sells monuments from its second tier.

  • Reverting an S+ hero no longer shows a second copy of it in the client.

  • Lucky Roulette keeps your balances in step with the server, so it no longer sends the game back to the title after a few spins.

  • Anniversary boxes you already opened no longer reappear as unusable empty items in the bag after feeding the cake.

  • A Skip button in campaign and tower fights that fast-forwards to the end (arena, guild war and replays keep their own skip), on iOS and Android.

  • On the home page the ad button becomes Skip, shown only when your team has at least 1.5 times the enemy power. The server works the powers out from your last team and the stage you are on, and pressing it starts the battle and fast-forwards it.

  • The cake, arena, dungeon and hard campaign answers no longer resend old empty rows.

  • Event exchanges that pay hero experience, such as the 100k XP of the anniversary shop, now credit it. Past purchases are paid out.

Long termGoal

  • Rebuild the game client from the ground up, so new features no longer depend on patching the original app on each platform.
