Select 10 for the Elite Hero Cards.

Ten elites of the element you choose in one press, without the ten elite animations, on iOS and Android.

NewFixUnder the hood

  • Select 10 next to Select in the element window of the Elite Hero Cards: it uses ten cards for ten A grade elites of the chosen element, opened at once without their elite animations.

  • The hero cards of the VIP rewards (levels 12 to 16) were credited to a balance the game never showed: they now reach your Elite Hero Cards, those already claimed included.

  • Auto Equip picks the pieces the game itself calls better (by CP, the element bonus included): the red dot and the button agree.

  • Taking a piece another princess wears now swaps them: it no longer shows on both, and the piece it replaces goes to her.

Select 10

The element window of the Elite Hero Cards has a Select 10 button to the right of Select.

  1. Open the Elite Hero Cards in the Tavern and choose an element.
  2. Press Select 10: ten cards are used for ten A grade elites of that element.
  3. The ten cards open at once, without the elite animation of each one.
  • Select still pulls one, with its animation.
  • With fewer than ten cards, nothing is used.
